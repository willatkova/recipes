### Marie Biscuit Slices
---
#### Ingredients
- 1 Packet Marie Biscuits
- 175 Gr Butter
- 250 Gr Icing Sugar
- 2 Tbls Cocoa
- 1 Tsp Vanilla Essence
- 1 Egg
---
#### Directions
- Break the biscuits
- Mix the egg & vanilla essense in a bowl
- Melt the butter on low heat
- Add icing sugar & cocoa
- Turn off the heat
- Add egg mixture
- Gently add biscuits
- Pour into greased dish
- Allow to cool
