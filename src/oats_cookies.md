### Oats Cookies
---
#### Ingredients
- 250 Gr Butter
- 1 Cup Sugar
- 1 Egg
- 2 Tsp Vanilla Essence
- 2 Tbsp Custard Powder
- 1 Cup Oats
- 3 Cups Flour
- 1 Tsp Baking Soda
- Pinch of Salt
---
#### Directions
- Mix butter & sugar till creamy
- Add egg & vanilla essence
- Mix for 2 minutes
- Mix in custard, oats, baking soda & salt
- Add flour gradually until you have a soft dough
- Roll into balls
- Bake at 180 degrees for 12 minutes
