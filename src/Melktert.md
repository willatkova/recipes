### Melktert 
#### Makes 2 tarts
---
#### Ingredients - Crust
- 125g Soft Butter
- 1 Egg
- 3 Tsp Sugar - Heaped
- 1 Cup Flour
- 1/4 Tsp Baking Powder
- Pinch of Salt
---
#### Directions - Crust
```
Mix well until it forms a dough.
Press into the tart plate.
```
