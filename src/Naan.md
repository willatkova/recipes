### Naan Bread - Sourdough
---
#### Ingredients
- 270gr AP Flour
- 60gr Discard / Starter
- 60gr Greek Yoghurt
- 10gr (2tsp) Baking Powder
- 8gr (1tsp) Salt
120gr Water - Room Temperature
---
#### Directions
- Mix all together
- Form a smooth ball with your hands
- Use a bench scraper to make 6-10 pieces
- Heat a cast iron pan to medium heat
- Brush with oil
- Roll naan out flat
- Put naan in pan, 1 minute or until it bubbles or edges brown
- Flip and cook on other side
- Brush naan with butter and sprinkle on garlic
